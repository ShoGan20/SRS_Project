# Project Phoenix - Kickoff Meeting

**Date:** 2 June 2026
**Attendees:** Priya Nair (Head of Customer Experience, sponsor), Tom Becker (IT Delivery Manager), Aisha Khan (Billing Operations Lead), Marco Rossi (Solution Architect)

## Purpose
Replace the phone- and paper-based billing enquiry process with a customer self-service web portal for our residential energy customers.

## Problems today
- Call centre handles ~40,000 billing calls per month; average wait time is 14 minutes.
- Customers cannot see bills older than 3 months without calling.
- Meter readings are submitted by phone and keyed in manually, causing ~6% error rate.

## Goals (Priya)
- Reduce billing-related calls by 30% within 6 months of launch.
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) for billing to rise from 3.1 to 4.0 out of 5.

## Discussion
- Customers must be able to register with their account number and email, and log in securely. Tom: MFA is mandatory per the security policy.
- View and download the last 24 months of bills as PDF.
- Submit meter readings online, with validation against the previous reading (flag readings that are more than 3x the usual usage).
- Pay a bill online. **Decision: card payments only in phase 1**; direct debit set-up deferred to phase 2 (Aisha agreed).
- Marco: the portal must integrate with SAP IS-U (billing system) via the existing API gateway. No direct DB access.
- Portal must support **5,000 concurrent users** at month-end peak (Priya).
- Mobile-friendly web; native app is out of scope.

- Aisha to share list of bill types and edge cases.
- Marco to confirm SAP API rate limits.
