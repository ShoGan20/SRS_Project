Phoenix - Security review notes
23 June 2026
Present: Tom Becker, Leila Haddad (Information Security), Marco Rossi

- Leila: login must use MFA (email one-time code or authenticator app). Lock the account for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts.
- Sessions time out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
- Card payments must go through the PCI-DSS compliant payment provider (hosted payment page). The portal must never store card numbers.
- All personal data encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
- Audit log of logins, payments and meter reading submissions, kept for 7 years.
- Penetration test required before go-live.
- Open: GDPR data retention period for closed accounts - Legal to advise.
